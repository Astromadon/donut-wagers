Platform guide

A clear path from
verification to payout.

DonutWagers keeps every important step visible so players know exactly what is happening to their account and wager.

01

Create your identity

Sign in through Discord, then link your Minecraft account using the verification flow. Your Minecraft UUID—not a typed username—is the permanent identity.

02

Fund your wallet

Start a deposit request and pay the exact amount to the verified DonutWagers bot before the timer expires. Confirmed deposits are credited automatically.

03

Create or join

Choose Crystal PvP or Sword, your region, match settings, and wager amount. Entry funds are locked as soon as you commit to a match.

04

Play and report

Use the unique match room to copy your opponent’s Minecraft name, follow the displayed rules, and submit the result when the duel ends.

05

Resolve and settle

Matching reports settle automatically. Conflicting reports create a staff dispute where proof can be reviewed before an outcome is finalized.
