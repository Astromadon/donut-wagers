HOW IT WORKS

Secure Duels, verified escrow.

Every critical step is checked on the server—not by a button in your browser.

01Verify

Link a Minecraft account only through the configured verification flow.

02Create

Set CPvP or Sword, 1v1 region, rules, lead, and amount.

03Deposit

Send the amount to DonutWagersBot; the transaction must be verified.

04Play

A wager is ready only after both player deposits verify.
