Platform rules

Use only one verified Minecraft account per DonutWagers account.
Never share account access, verification codes, or session credentials.
Deposits count only after server-side verification of the transfer to DonutWagersBot.
Creating a match does not prove a deposit; both deposits must verify before a match is ready.
Disputes and result handling require recorded evidence and staff review.
