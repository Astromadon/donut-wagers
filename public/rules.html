Match standards

Compete fairly.
Keep proof.

These baseline rules protect both players. The settings displayed inside a match room are part of that match’s agreement.

01

Verified accounts only

Players must compete using the Minecraft account linked to their DonutWagers profile. Account sharing or impersonation is prohibited.

02

Honor match settings

Game mode, region, first-to target, round lead, totem amount, and wager are locked when the match begins.

03

Report honestly

Submit a win only after the duel is complete. False results may lead to an overturned match, balance correction, suspension, or ban.

04

Preserve evidence

Record the duel when possible. Screenshots, full clips, chat, and timestamps help staff resolve conflicting reports.

05

No exploits

Do not abuse bugs, duplicate matches, evade locked balances, manipulate deposits, or coordinate results between accounts.

06

Respect staff decisions

Use Contact Staff from the match room for a dispute. Staff may overturn results when reliable evidence supports the decision.
