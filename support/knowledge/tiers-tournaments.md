# PvP tiers and tournaments
category: tiers

## PvP tiers
Tier badges show a player's MCTiers ranking (mctiers.com) for one gamemode. A match shows only the ranking for its own gamemode: Sword matches show the Sword tier, Crystal PvP matches show the MCTiers Vanilla tier. Tiers go from HT1 (High Tier 1, the best) through LT1, HT2, LT2 down to LT5. Players cannot set or edit their own ranking, and staff tags never show a tier.

Rankings are read from MCTiers for your verified Minecraft account ID, right after you verify and then about every 12 hours. "Unranked" means MCTiers has no ranking for that gamemode. "Retired" means the player retired in that gamemode; the badge then shows their peak tier with an R in front. An "out of date" note means the last check failed or is old; the last known ranking is kept. Finished matches show the tier each player had when the match was played. You can ask for a refresh once every 15 minutes from your profile. A ranking does not prove who owns an account; only Minecraft verification does.

## Tournaments
Open Tournaments to see events. Registration needs a verified Minecraft account and the age confirmation. Entry is free; the winner prize is shown on each event. Formats are a bracket or a leaderboard over a set number of hours.
