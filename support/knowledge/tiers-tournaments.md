# PvP tiers and tournaments
category: tiers

## PvP tiers
Tier badges show a player's MCPVP ranking for one kit, such as Sword or Crystal. A match shows only the ranking for its own kit. Formats such as HT1 (High Tier 1), MT3 (Mid Tier 3) or LT4 (Low Tier 4) come directly from MCPVP. Players cannot set or edit their own ranking, and staff tags never show a tier.

Rankings are connected only after MCPVP has approved access. Until then badges show as unavailable. "Unranked" means MCPVP has no ranking for that kit. An "out of date" note means the last check is older than usual or failed; the last known ranking is kept. A verified Minecraft account is needed for rankings. You can ask for a refresh once every 15 minutes from your profile.

## Tournaments
Open Tournaments to see events. Registration needs a verified Minecraft account and the age confirmation. Entry is free; the winner prize is shown on each event. Formats are a bracket or a leaderboard over a set number of hours.
