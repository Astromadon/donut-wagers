# Getting help and troubleshooting
category: troubleshooting

## Talking to a person
- In AI Support, press "Talk to a person". Staff see your request and this conversation in the admin panel and reply on Discord.
- For a match, use "Contact staff" in the match room to open a private Discord ticket.
- The DonutWagers Discord server: discord.gg/pnBcnxKPF6

## Reporting a bug
Describe what you did, what you expected and what happened, the page, and the time. Use "Talk to a person" so staff receive it. Never include passwords or codes.

## Common problems
- "The bot is offline": deposits, withdrawals and verification wait for the bot. Try again later.
- "Switched off right now": the owner has paused that part of the site for a while.
- A page shows old numbers: refresh the page. The site updates live while you are connected.
- "Too many requests": wait the number of seconds shown, then try again.
- Sign-in loops back to the homepage: allow cookies for donutwagers.com and try again; the sign-in link expires after 10 minutes.
- You cannot join a match: check your verified account, age confirmation, break status and available balance.
