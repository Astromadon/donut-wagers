# Getting started
category: account

## Signing in
DonutWagers uses "Log in with Discord". The site only asks Discord for your username, ID and avatar. It never sees your Discord password, email, messages or servers. If sign-in fails, try again from the homepage; the sign-in link is only valid for 10 minutes. Make sure your browser allows cookies for donutwagers.com.

## What you need before playing
1. Sign in with Discord.
2. Verify your Minecraft account (Profile, then Connections, then Verify).
3. Confirm you are 18 or older when the site asks.
4. Deposit DonutSMP money in the Wallet.

## Parts of the site that are paused
The owner can switch parts of the site off for a while, for example matchmaking, deposits, withdrawals, tournament sign-ups, snipes, match chat or new accounts. When a part is off, the page says it is "switched off right now". Nothing in progress is lost: running matches carry on and payments already sent are still credited.

## Currency
DonutWagers uses DonutSMP in-game money only. Amounts can be typed as 500k, 5m, 1.5m or as full numbers.
