# Wallet: balance, deposits and withdrawals
category: balance

## Balance
"Available" is what you can use. "Locked" is money held for your open or active matches and for withdrawals being sent. Locked money is returned when a match is cancelled or expires.

## Deposits
1. Open Wallet, choose Deposit and enter an amount. The minimum is 100,000.
2. The site shows a /pay command. Send exactly that amount from your verified Minecraft account within 6 minutes.
3. DonutSMP keeps 2% of every /pay, so the amount to send is a little higher than your deposit. Example: to deposit 5,000,000 you send 5,102,041, and 5,000,000 is added to your balance.
4. The bot confirms the payment and your balance updates automatically.

Deposits only work while the bot is online. You can have one deposit waiting at a time.

## A deposit did not arrive
- It must come from your verified account, and the amount must be exactly the one shown.
- Payments sent after the 6 minutes, or with a different amount, are not lost: they are kept for staff to review and credit. Contact staff with the time and amount.

## Withdrawals
- The minimum is 15,000,000. The amount is locked when you request it.
- The bot pays it to your verified Minecraft account. DonutSMP keeps 2%, so about 98% arrives (15,000,000 withdrawn means about 14,700,000 received).
- If the bot cannot confirm the payment, the withdrawal goes to a staff check. The amount stays reserved, and a payout is never sent twice.
- You can withdraw while taking a break.

## Transaction history
Profile, then Transactions, lists deposits, withdrawals and match results. The Wallet page shows recent activity.
