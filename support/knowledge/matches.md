# Matches
category: matches

## Creating a match
Press Create Match and choose:
- Mode: Crystal PvP or Sword.
- Region: EU or NAE.
- First to: 1, 2, 3 (win by 2) or 5 (win by 2).
- Opponent's round lead: 0 to 4 rounds, always fewer than the first-to target.
- Entry: at least 1,000,000.

Both players put in the same entry. The platform keeps 10% of both entries, so the winner receives 1.8 times the entry (a 10M entry pays 18M). Your entry is locked when you create the match. You can have up to 5 open matches at once.

An open match closes after 30 minutes if nobody joins, and your entry comes back. The creator can cancel an open match at any time before someone joins.

## Joining
Press Join match on an open match. Your entry is locked straight away. The other player's name stays hidden until both players are ready. A snipe reveals who created an open match and their record; every account starts with 3 snipes.

## Ready up and fight
1. Both players press Ready within 15 minutes, or the match is cancelled and both entries are returned.
2. Names, the duel command and match chat unlock.
3. Join the server, send the duel command and play the agreed terms.
4. Both players report the winner in the match room, and can add the final score. The score is shown only if both reports give the same one.

## Results and disputes
- If both reports agree, the match settles and the winner is paid immediately.
- If the reports differ, the match becomes disputed and staff decide.
- If only one player reports and the other does not answer within 24 hours, the match goes to staff.
- After both players joined, cancelling needs both players to agree.
- Keep evidence: recordings, screenshots and the time of the fight help staff decide. Use "Contact staff" in the match room to open a private Discord ticket for that match.

## Match page, history and rematches
- Every match has its own page with the settings, a progress timeline, both players, the rules and the match chat. Anyone signed in can open it; only the two players and staff can chat or act.
- Open any player’s profile and press View Matches to see their match history; every match there opens in full, including old ones.
- After a settled match, both players can press Rematch (same entry) or Double (twice the entry) within 30 minutes. A new match with the same settings starts at ready-up only when both pick the same option, and both entries are locked.
- Matches are anonymous: you can’t see who you are playing until both players have pressed Ready. If a match is cancelled before it starts, it stays anonymous. A snipe is the only way to see an open match’s creator.

## Statistics
Your statistics show wins, losses, win rate, profit, streaks and tournament results. You can feature one of your winning wager matches on your profile.
