# Shop, cosmetics and cashback
category: rewards

## The shop
The Shop sells snipes, Streak Protectors, titles and profile borders. Prices are in your DonutWagers balance. Only your available balance can be spent; money locked in a match cannot. Every purchase asks you to confirm and shows the price and your balance afterwards. A purchase is final. If something went wrong, press Talk to a person.

## Titles and borders
Titles and profile borders are cosmetic. Buy them once, then equip one title and one border from the shop or the Inventory tab. They show on your profile, statistics, the leaderboard, search and match pages. You can preview any title or border on your own avatar before buying. A title such as HT1 is only a title. It is not an MCTiers ranking, and your real tier badges always come from MCTiers.

## Streak Protectors
A Streak Protector keeps your win streak when you lose. If you have one, protection is on, and you have at least one win in a row, your next loss uses one protector and your streak stays where it was. The loss still counts in your record and the entry is not refunded. Your next win adds to the streak. You can switch automatic protection off in the Inventory tab. If staff later change that result to a win, the protector is returned.

## Snipes from the shop
Snipes you buy go into the same snipe balance you already have. Use one on an open match to see who created it before you join.

## Cashback
Cashback gives part of your own stake back after finished wager matches. Every 100M you stake earns the cashback amount shown on the Cashback page, and anything over a step carries forward. Only your own entry counts. Cancelled or refunded matches, tournaments, deposits, withdrawals and staff adjustments don't count. Playing the same opponent many times a day is limited and checked by staff. You need a verified Minecraft account to claim. Cashback can be switched off by the owner; while it is off, nothing new counts, and anything already earned stays.
