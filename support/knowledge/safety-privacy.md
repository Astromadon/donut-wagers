# Account security, age, breaks and privacy
category: security

## Keeping your account safe
- Staff and this assistant never ask for passwords, Discord tokens or Minecraft login codes. Do not share them with anyone.
- Only sign in through the "Log in with Discord" button on donutwagers.com.
- Settings has "Sign out other devices" if you think someone else used your account.
- Verification codes only work from your own Minecraft account, so do not send your code to anyone else.

## Age
You must be 18 or older, and wagering must be allowed where you live. The site asks you to confirm this before you play or deposit.

## Taking a break
Settings, then "Take a break", pauses creating and joining matches and deposits for 1, 7, 30 or 180 days. A break can be made longer but not shorter. You can still withdraw.

## Your data
Settings, then "Your data", lets you download everything stored about your account or delete your account. Withdraw your balance first; a balance below the withdrawal minimum is lost on deletion. The privacy policy is at donutwagers.com/privacy.

## Rules
- Play on the Minecraft account linked to your profile. No account sharing or impersonation.
- Match settings are fixed once a match is created.
- Report results honestly. False reports can lead to an overturned match, a balance correction or a ban.
- No exploits, deposit manipulation or arranged results between accounts.
The full rules are at donutwagers.com/rules.
