# Minecraft account verification
category: verification

## How to verify
1. Open Profile, then Connections, and press Verify.
2. Enter the exact Minecraft name you play with on DonutSMP.
3. The site shows a six-digit code. It works for 10 minutes and only from that Minecraft account.
4. In DonutSMP, send a private message to the bot: /msg DonutWagersBot CODE
5. The site confirms automatically once the bot sees your message.

You can request at most 5 codes in 10 minutes. Each DonutWagers account can link one Minecraft account, and each Minecraft account can belong to one DonutWagers account.

## Verification is not working
- Check the name is exactly right, and that you sent the message from that same account.
- The code may have expired after 10 minutes. Request a new one.
- The bot must be online. If the site says the bot is offline, try again later.
- If the Minecraft account is already linked to another DonutWagers account, it cannot be linked again. Contact staff if you think that is a mistake.

## Changed your Minecraft name?
Open Profile, then Connections, and press "Update name". Verify again with the new name from the same Minecraft account. Pending payouts follow the new name.

## Disconnecting
You can disconnect Minecraft in Connections, but not while you have open or active matches or a withdrawal in progress.
