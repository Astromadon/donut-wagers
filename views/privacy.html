Privacy policy

What DonutWagers collects, why, who can see it, and how to get it back or delete it.

Last updated 25 November 2026

In short

  • You sign in with Discord. We receive your Discord ID, username, and avatar. We do not ask for your email address, password, messages, or server list.
  • We store what the site needs to run: your Minecraft name, balance, matches, match chat, deposits, and withdrawals.
  • There is no advertising and no analytics tracking, and we do not sell your data.
  • Other signed-in players can see your profile, results, and profit.
  • You can download your data or delete your account in Settings.

Who this covers

This policy is for the DonutWagers website and the DonutWagersBot account on DonutSMP. “We” means the people who run DonutWagers. DonutWagers is not affiliated with DonutSMP, Mojang, Microsoft, or Discord, and their own policies apply to their services.

Who can see it

  • Everyone, including people who are not signed in: the terms of open matches and site-wide totals. These do not name you.
  • Signed-in players: your Discord ID, username and avatar, Minecraft name, tags, PvP tiers, rank, wins and losses, streaks, profit and losses, amounts played, tournament results, your matches once both players are revealed (opponent, score, result, entry and prize), the match you feature, and when you joined. Finished matches keep your name and tier as they were when played, until you delete your account.
  • People with a match link: both players’ names once the match has started or been cancelled.
  • Your opponent and staff: the match chat.
  • Only you: your AI Support conversations. Staff see one only after you press “Talk to a person” in it.
  • Staff: what they need to handle disputes, payments, and bans. Your exact balance and your deposits and withdrawals are not shown to other players.

Services we use

  • Discord handles sign-in. If you contact staff about a match, we create a private Discord channel with the match link and your Discord and Minecraft names; the other player in the match is added to it. If you are banned here, your Discord ID and the reason may be sent to Discord to ban you from our server.
  • Railway hosts the website and its database.
  • mc-heads.net supplies Minecraft skin heads. Our server asks for them with your Minecraft account ID; your browser does not contact it.
  • DonutSMP and Minecraft: verification messages and payments happen in game through DonutWagersBot, so the server’s operators can see them.
  • Anthropic provides the AI that answers AI Support questions, once it is switched on. It receives your question, earlier messages in that conversation, and, when the question is about your account, a short summary of your own balance, matches, or wallet activity. It does not receive your Discord ID or other players’ data.
  • MCTiers: we look up your PvP rankings there with your Minecraft account ID, and check them again regularly.

Your browser loads avatars from Discord, so Discord can see your IP address. Links to Discord, YouTube, X, and TikTok open those services only when you press them. We may also share information when the law requires it, or to protect players from fraud or harm.

Cookies and browser storage

A referral link sets a cookie (dw_ref) for up to 30 days with the referrer’s code. It is only read if you create an account, and is then removed.

We use only what the site needs to work. dw_session keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. dw_oauth_state protects the sign-in step and lasts 10 minutes. Your browser also stores your background choice, selected game, and when you last opened your notifications.

How long we keep it

Account, match, and payment records stay while your account exists, because results and balances depend on them. Sessions end after 30 days or when you log out. Each match keeps its last 200 chat messages. AI Support conversations are deleted 30 days after your last message, or sooner if you delete them; one that staff are still answering is kept until they have answered it, and answered requests are deleted after 90 days. Backups may hold older copies until they are replaced.

Your choices

  • Download: Settings has a file with the data stored about your account.
  • Delete: Settings can delete your account. Finish your matches and withdrawals first. Any remaining balance below the withdrawal minimum is lost.
  • Sessions: Settings can sign you out of your other devices.
  • Correct or ask: contact us to correct something, ask a question, or object to how your data is used. Depending on where you live, you may have more rights, including complaining to a data protection authority.

Deleting removes your Discord ID, name, avatar, Minecraft link, favorites, and sessions, and clears your chat messages. Match results, payments, bans, and the action log stay under “Deleted player” so that other players’ results and balances remain correct. A payment staff have not resolved yet keeps its Minecraft name until they do. Discord channels created for staff help, and copies in backups, are not removed automatically; ask us about those.

Keeping it safe

Connections are encrypted, session tokens and codes are hashed, access is limited by role, and staff actions on balances and results are logged. No system is perfectly secure, so tell us quickly if you notice a problem.
