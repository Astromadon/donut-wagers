From verification to payout

What happens to your account and your wager at each step, and what to do when something goes wrong.

  1. Verify your account

    Log in with Discord, enter your Minecraft name, and send the one-time code to the DonutWagers bot in a private message on DonutSMP. The bot checks who sent it, so nobody can claim your account.

  2. Fund your wallet

    Start a deposit and pay the bot the exact amount shown within six minutes. DonutSMP keeps 2% of every payment, so the amount shown is a little higher than your deposit and the full deposit reaches your balance. The minimum is 100,000. Confirmed payments are added to your balance automatically.

  3. Create or join a match

    Choose Crystal PvP or Sword, EU or NAE, the first-to target, and the entry. Your entry is locked the moment you commit. An open match that nobody joins within 30 minutes closes and returns the entry.

  4. Ready up and fight

    Names stay hidden until both players ready up. If both aren’t ready within 15 minutes, the match is cancelled and both entries are returned. A snipe reveals an open match’s creator and record before you join. Once both are ready, the room shows the duel command and opens the chat.

  5. Report and get paid

    Each player reports the winner. When both reports match, the winner receives both entries minus a 10% platform fee. When they don’t, or only one player reports within 24 hours, the match becomes a dispute and staff review the proof.

  6. Withdraw

    Withdrawals start at 15,000,000 and are paid to your verified Minecraft account by the bot. DonutSMP keeps 2% of the payment, so you receive 98% of what you withdraw. If a payout can’t be confirmed, the amount stays reserved until staff have checked it.
