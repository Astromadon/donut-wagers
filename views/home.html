From lobby to payout

Read the full guide

  1. Verify

    Log in with Discord and prove your Minecraft account with a one-time code.

  2. Deposit

    Pay the DonutWagers bot in game. Your balance updates by itself.

  3. Fight

    Join a match, ready up, and copy the duel command straight from the room.

  4. Get paid

    When both reports agree, the winner is paid. If they don’t, staff decide.
