Compete fairly. Keep proof.

These rules apply to every match. The settings shown in a match room are part of that match’s agreement.

Verified accounts only

Play on the Minecraft account linked to your profile. Sharing accounts or impersonating another player is prohibited.

Honor the match settings

Game mode, region, first-to target, round lead, and entry are fixed once the match is created.

Report honestly

Report a winner only after the duel ends. False reports can lead to an overturned match, a balance correction, or a ban.

Keep evidence

Record the duel when you can. Full clips, screenshots, and timestamps help staff settle conflicting reports.

No exploits

Don’t abuse bugs, evade locked balances, manipulate deposits, or arrange results between accounts.

Use Contact Staff

Raise disputes from the match room. Staff may overturn a result when reliable evidence supports it, and that decision stands.

Check the terms before you join

Every open match lists its mode, region, target, and entry.
